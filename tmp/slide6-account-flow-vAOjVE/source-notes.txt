Implemented behavior summarized on slide 6:
- Registration accepts email, username, and password; validates identity; hashes the password with bcrypt; persists the user and welcome alert; returns an access token; and leaves email verification pending.
- The account-email service creates a signed verification token that expires after 60 minutes and sends the link over SMTP to Mailpit in local development.
- The frontend verification route submits the token to the API, which validates purpose and expiry and records email_verified_at.
- Registration does not block the user from signing in while email verification is pending.
